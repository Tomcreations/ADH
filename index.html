ADH

ADH for Aestik by Tom Creations. It gives players one place to install, organize, and launch mods, and it is still being actively developed.

What it is

ADH is a Windows mod loader for Aestik. It is the tool people use when they want a single, organized place for managing mods instead of dealing with scattered files and manual setup.

What it is for

It is for players who want to keep their Aestik mods easy to install, easy to find, and easy to launch. That makes it useful as a simple home for modded play.

Who made it

ADH is by Tom Creations. It is presented here as a clear download page and a straightforward public home for the project.

Who it is for

ADH is for Aestik players who want a practical modding tool, and for mod creators who want their work to live inside a clear, organized loader experience.

Short verdict

ADH looks like a focused, useful project with a clear purpose: make Aestik modding easier to manage and easier to use. It is best presented as a released but still-growing modding tool.
