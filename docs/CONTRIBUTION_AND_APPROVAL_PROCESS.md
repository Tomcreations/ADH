# Contribution and Approval Process

Thank you for your interest in contributing to ADH.

## Contributing to ADH

Anyone may submit changes, improvements, bug fixes, or new features through a Pull Request.

Submitting a Pull Request does not guarantee it will be accepted. All contributions are reviewed before being merged into ADH.

Reasons a contribution may be rejected include:

* Malicious code or security concerns.
* Broken functionality.
* Poor code quality.
* Features that do not fit the project's goals.
* Incomplete or untested changes.

## Questions

If you have questions about contributing, a Pull Request, or a decision regarding your contribution, you can contact us on Discord.

Discord Username: tomcreations_owner

We are generally open to discussion and are willing to review additional information when needed.

## Community Mods

Community mod support is planned but is not currently available.

In the future, users will be able to upload mods through the ADH application.

When community mods become available:

* Uploaded mods may be removed at any time.
* All new mods will receive an Unofficial tag by default.
* Unofficial mods have not been reviewed by the ADH team.
* Mods may later be reviewed and marked as Approved.
* Mods may also be removed if they violate guidelines, contain harmful content, or are otherwise unsuitable for the platform.

Approval does not guarantee that a mod is safe, bug-free, or compatible with every system.

Users install community mods at their own risk.

## Final Decisions

The ADH team reserves the right to accept, reject, remove, or modify contributions and community content when necessary for the health, security, and quality of the project.
